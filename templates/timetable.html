TIME TABLE GENERATOR

How to Use:

  1. Configure the timetable settings (days, periods, start time, etc.)
  2. Click "Generate Timetable" to create the empty timetable
  3. Fill in the subject details (subject, teacher, room, color)
  4. Click on any time slot to add the subject
  5. Click "Download as PDF" to save your timetable
